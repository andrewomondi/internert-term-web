Web 2.0

Web 2.0 refers to the second generation of the World Wide Web, characterized by interactive content, social media, and user participation. Web 2.0 works by encouraging user participation and collaboration. Instead of just reading, users can now write, share, and interact through social media, blogs, and wikis. This shift made the web dynamic and community-driven, changing it into a platform for communication and creativity.
