Web 3.0

Web 3.0 is the next evolution of the web, focusing on decentralization, blockchain technologies, and intelligent applications. Web 3.0 works by decentralizing control and adding meaning to data. It uses blockchain and semantic technologies to give users ownership of their information and enable smarter searches. The goal is a web that is more personalized, secure, and user-controlled
