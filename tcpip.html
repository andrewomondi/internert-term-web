TCP/IP

TCP/IP is the communication protocol that defines how data is transmitted across the Internet. TCP/IP works as the foundation of how computers communicate over the Internet. The IP part handles addressing, making sure data knows where to go, while TCP breaks information into packets, sends them reliably, and reassembles them at the destination. Together, they ensure that when you send an email, stream a video, or browse a website, the data travels across networks accurately and in the right order
