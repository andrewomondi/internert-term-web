Web 4.0

Web 4.0 is the envisioned future of the web, focusing on intelligent interaction, seamless connectivity, and integration with advanced technologies like AI and IoT. Web 4.0 works by integrating artificial intelligence and smart devices into everyday web use. It focuses on machine-to-machine communication, predictive services, and personalization. This makes the web feel more intelligent, anticipating user needs and connecting seamlessly with the Internet of Things.
